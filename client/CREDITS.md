# Credits

## UniGo logo

`brand/Logo-UniGo01.png` (full logo with tagline) and `brand/Logo-UniGo02.png` (round icon) belong to the UniGo project. The transparent versions, wordmark, favicon and PWA icons are generated from them by `scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs`, without changing the design or colours.

## Home service card pictures

`brand/services/ride.png`, `carpool.png`, `food.png` and `service.png` are the **UniGo project's own images**. `scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs` makes the WebP files in `src/assets/services/` from them (trimmed, resized for the cards at 1x and 2x, and with large enclosed see-through areas filled with white so they look the same on dark cards), without changing the design.

No images, logos or artwork from Pathao or any other app are used.
